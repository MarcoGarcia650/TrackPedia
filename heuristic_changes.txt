TrackPedia Project - Heuristic Evaluation & Improvement Report

Nielsen’s 10 Heuristics Assessment:

List of Heuristic Violations Identified & Matched Values:

1. Violation: Missing feedback during background geocoding/searching.
   Heuristic: 1. Visibility of System Status
   Reason: Users were left wondering if the search was actually working or if the app was frozen when no immediate local results appeared.

2. Violation: Technical ID strings shown instead of track names in URL or headers.
   Heuristic: 2. Match Between System and Real World
   Reason: Displaying database UIDs or internal "osm-123" strings confuses users who just want to see "Laguna Seca".

3. Violation: No way to reset the checklist once items were checked.
   Heuristic: 3. User Control and Freedom
   Reason: Users who finished one track day and wanted to reuse the list had to manually uncheck 15+ items or clear browser cache.

4. Violation: Inconsistent button styling between the Home and Planning pages.
   Heuristic: 4. Consistency and Standards
   Reason: Mixed use of pill-shaped and square buttons made the app feel fragmented and less professional.

5. Violation: Users could accidentally book for a past date.
   Heuristic: 5. Error Prevention
   Reason: Allowing date selection in the past leads to failed transactions and user frustration.

6. Violation: Forcing users to remember which tracks they had previously compared.
   Heuristic: 6. Recognition Rather Than Recall
   Reason: There was no persistent "Comparison" view or history, meaning users had to re-search tracks multiple times.

7. Violation: No shortcut to filter for specific vehicle types (Cars vs Bikes).
   Heuristic: 7. Flexibility and Efficiency of Use
   Reason: Experienced track riders had to scroll through mixed results, making the workflow inefficient.

8. Violation: Cluttered result cards with excessive metadata (lat/long, internal tags).
   Heuristic: 8. Aesthetic and Minimalist Design
   Reason: Excessive data overwhelmed the user's ability to quickly compare prices and locations.

9. Violation: Cryptic Firebase error messages on failed login/booking.
   Heuristic: 9. Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors
   Reason: Showing "auth/user-not-found" is technical jargon; it doesn't help the user understand they need to sign up.

10. Violation: No guidance on what gear is actually required for a beginner.
    Heuristic: 10. Help and Documentation
    Reason: New users were left searching external sites for requirement lists, breaking the app's value proposition as a one-stop shop.

---

Addressed Heuristic Violations & Implementation Details:

1. Visibility of System Status
   Address: Added a 'Searching...' status indicator with a Loader2 spinner in the search bar. Implemented a 100% progress circle in the Checklist page that updates in real-time as items are ticked.

2. Match Between System and Real World
   Address: Replaced technical descriptions with domain-specific language (e.g., "Paddock Supplies", "Vehicle Prep", "Tech Inspection"). Used real track imagery and localized names from OpenStreetMap Nominatim.

3. User Control and Freedom
   Address: Implemented a "Reset" button for the checklist with a confirmation dialog to prevent accidental data loss while allowing users to restart their prep cycle.

4. Consistency and Standards
   Address: Standardized all primary actions to use the 'brand' blue color and Lucide-react icons. Applied a unified 'Navbar' across all views (Home, Planning, Results).

5. Error Prevention
   Address: Integrated 'react-datepicker' with constraints to prevent invalid date ranges. Added a confirmation prompt if a user tries to checkout without being signed in.

6. Recognition Rather Than Recall
   Address: Created a "Compare" feature that saves tracks to a temporary list, allowing users to view them side-by-side without re-searching. Added a "Recommended" sort that defaults to the most relevant items.

7. Flexibility and Efficiency of Use
   Address: Added "Vehicle Type" toggles (Car vs Motorcycle) across the Search and Checklist pages to instantly filter out irrelevant data for the user's specific hobby.

8. Aesthetic and Minimalist Design
   Address: Redesigned Result Cards to focus strictly on: Image, Name, Location, and Price. Moved secondary details (hotels, latitude) into sub-views or tooltips.

9. Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors
   Address: Replaced technical alerts with human-readable messages (e.g., "No tracks found - Try adjusting your search filters or searching for California").

10. Help and Documentation
    Address: Added "Rookie Tip" callouts and a dedicated "Track Day Checklist" page to guide beginners through the necessary preparations and documentation.
